In short. Lockwright has no accounts, ads or analytics and does not sell any data about you. The game works offline. Its only network use is downloading voice packs (see below); without them the game shows subtitles.

What stays on your device. The game saves your progress (for example coins, tools, opened locks, unlocked showcase notes) and your settings (for example sound and haptics) on your device. These files never leave your device through us, and we have no access to them. To delete them, uninstall the game or clear its data in your system settings.

Voice downloads (the only network use). Subtitles work in every game language straight away. English voice recordings are included in the game; voices in the game's language (by default your phone's language) are downloaded in the background on first launch, and the game shows the download size while it happens. You download other languages yourself in Settings, where you can also turn automatic downloads off and remove downloaded voices. A download is an ordinary HTTPS request to the server that hosts the game's files. The game sends no identifier, account or gameplay data with it, and uses no cookies or analytics. Like any internet connection, however, it reveals your IP address and basic technical request data (such as the time and the file requested) to the server. These may appear in the hosting provider's logs: currently [HOSTING PROVIDER — GitHub, and Cloudflare after a move to our own hosting; to be confirmed], and we do not use those logs to identify players. You can remove a downloaded pack in the game's Settings, by clearing the game's data or by uninstalling it.

System backups. If device backup is on (for example iCloud on iPhone or Google backup on Android), the system may copy the game's files along with your other data. Apple or Google keeps that backup in your account under their own terms. We can't see it.

Purchases. If the game offers an in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We don't receive your payment details or account data. The game only checks, on your device, whether the purchase was made.

Crash reports. The game has no crash-reporting tools of its own. If you have agreed in your device settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may pass anonymised crash reports on to us. We use them only to fix bugs.
