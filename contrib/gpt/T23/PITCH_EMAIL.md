**Temat:** Lockwright: gra o ślusarzu, który najpierw słucha — i czasem odmawia

Dzień dobry,

przygotowujemy **Lockwright: Pogotowie Zamkowe**, mobilną grę logiczno-zręcznościową o uczciwym ślusarzu. Zamiast włamania jest pomoc sąsiadom; zamiast paska postępu — fotorealistyczny mechanizm, dźwięk i wyczucie pod dwoma kciukami.

## EN

**Subject:** Lockwright: an indie mobile puzzle about listening before opening

Hello,

We're working on **Lockwright: Locksmith on Call**, a tactile mobile puzzle game about an honest emergency locksmith. The focus isn't breaking in: it's helping neighbours get home, using two-thumb controls, photorealistic cutaways, sound and haptic feedback rather than floating progress bars.

We're also designing an “in the dark” mode built around listening and touch. A planned job-based layer explores another question: does the caller actually have the right to enter? Mrs Halina is waiting on the landing. So is her soup.

Would this be a fit for your coverage? I'd be glad to share a project overview and work-in-progress visuals. Every lock is fictional; it's a game, not a course.
