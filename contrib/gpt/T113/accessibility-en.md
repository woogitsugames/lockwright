# Accessibility in Lockwright

Open **Settings** to adjust the game to your needs.

- **Text size** adjusts the size of interface text.
- **Chalk markings** add visual markings that help indicate the mechanism's state. Their availability can vary by view and tutorial stage.
- **Reduce motion** reduces some transitions and animated hints, including in arrival scenes and dialogue. It does not necessarily remove all movement in the game.
- **Haptics** can be turned off or adjusted. If your device does not play vibration, use visual and audio feedback.
- **Sound and voices:** dialogue remains available as text when a recording is unavailable.

**Screen readers.** Full screen-reader support across all views cannot yet be confirmed. Some controls have accessibility labels, but the game needs further testing with screen readers on devices.
