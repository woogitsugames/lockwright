# T103 — szablon Voice Design

Propozycje dla przyszłych, fikcyjnych postaci. Nie wskazują rzeczywistych osób ani nie proszą o naśladowanie istniejącego głosu. Ten sam wybrany głos powinien pozostać rozpoznawalny we wszystkich językach gry. Przed użyciem trzeba wysłuchać próbek, sprawdzić zrozumiałość PL, EN, DE, FR i ES oraz zgodność z ustalonym profilem postaci. Nie wygenerowano ani nie wybrano żadnego głosu.

## Szablon

> A fictional adult [role and broad age range], speaking [language] with clear, natural diction. [Pitch and texture], [pace and rhythm]. Personality: [two or three distinct traits]. Delivery is [emotional restraint and warmth], appropriate for a short game dialogue. Close, clean recording without music or sound effects. Original voice; do not imitate any identifiable person.

Przed wpisaniem wieku należy sprawdzić kartę postaci. W razie postaci dziecięcej użyć dorosłej osoby lektorskiej o odpowiedniej interpretacji, zgodnie z praktyką projektu dla Hani.

## Pięć przykładów

1. **Przyszła bibliotekarka:** A fictional adult librarian in her fifties, speaking Polish with clear, natural diction. Mid-range voice with a dry, soft texture and measured pacing. Curious, patient and quietly amused. Warm but restrained, as if explaining a small discovery to a neighbor. Close, clean recording without music or effects. Original voice; do not imitate any identifiable person.

2. **Przyszły ogrodnik:** A fictional adult gardener in his sixties, speaking Polish with clear, natural diction. Low-mid voice with a light grain, unhurried rhythm and short pauses. Practical, gentle and mildly wry. Friendly, never theatrical. Close, clean recording without music or effects. Original voice; do not imitate any identifiable person.

3. **Przyszła techniczka:** A fictional adult building technician in her thirties, speaking Polish with clear, natural diction. Bright mid-range voice, crisp articulation and an even pace. Focused, considerate and quietly confident. Matter-of-fact delivery with a little warmth. Close, clean recording without music or effects. Original voice; do not imitate any identifiable person.

4. **Przyszły organizator wystawy:** A fictional adult exhibition organizer in his forties, speaking Polish with clear, natural diction. Light mid-range voice and a lively but controlled rhythm. Enthusiastic, thoughtful and slightly absent-minded. Conversational rather than announcer-like. Close, clean recording without music or effects. Original voice; do not imitate any identifiable person.

5. **Przyszła sąsiadka:** A fictional adult neighbor in her seventies, speaking Polish with clear, natural diction. Soft mid-low voice, steady pace and a gentle smile in the tone. Observant, reassuring and direct. Calm conversational delivery without exaggerating age. Close, clean recording without music or effects. Original voice; do not imitate any identifiable person.
