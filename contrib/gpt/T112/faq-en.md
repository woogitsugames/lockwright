# Lockwright — frequently asked questions

**How do I play?** Use the touchscreen and follow the tutorial. Choose a job, then check the client's details and authority to request the work. Complete the job or decline if the right to enter cannot be confirmed.

**Do I have to use vibration?** No. In Settings, you can turn haptics off or adjust their strength. Visual and audio feedback also convey information. Vibration depends on your device and its settings.

**Do I need an internet connection?** The game is intended to work offline. An internet connection may be needed to download an additional voice pack.

**How do I download voices?** Polish and English voices are intended to be included with the game. After choosing a language with an available pack, check the voice section in Settings: you can start a download and retry after an error. Pack availability depends on the game version. Dialogue remains available as text when a recording is unavailable.

**Does the game save my progress?** Progress and settings are stored on your device. Depending on your device settings, system backups may also include them.

**What happens to data when voices are downloaded?** The download server receives your IP address and technical connection data. See the privacy policy for the released version of the game for details.

**Where are the accessibility options?** Settings include text size, reduce motion, and chalk markings. The accessibility guide explains these options.
