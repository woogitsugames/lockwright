# "A game, not a course" — notice

**Short (first launch, stamp on the notepad sheet, D-21; store listing; end of every video longer than 10 s):**
A game, not a course. The locks are invented.

**Medium ("About" screen, home page):**
This is a game, not a course. The mechanisms are invented and simplified, and the locks carry no brands. A real
locksmith always checks that the customer has the right to enter.

**Full (website, Terms of Use section 7):**
Lockwright is a game. Every lock, padlock and safe in it is invented: none of them depicts a real product or brand, and
their proportions were changed on purpose so you can read them. The game does not teach you to open real locks and may
not be used to facilitate anything unlawful. The locksmith in the game, like a real one, first checks that the customer
has the right to enter, and says no when they don't. If you're locked out, call a locksmith or your building manager.

Implementation note: the notice must not be a blocking dialog (D-12, D-21) or ask for "I agree" — it is information,
not consent.
